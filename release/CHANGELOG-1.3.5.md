## 1.3.5

### Changes
- Separate on/off switches for the adhan notification and the iqama notification, so you can turn one off without affecting the other. Each switch sits next to its sound choice.
- On a fresh install the app now finds your own location automatically the first time it opens, instead of starting on Alexandria. If your location cannot be found (for example no internet) the app says so and lets you choose your country and city, and it tries again next time. Existing saved locations are not changed.
