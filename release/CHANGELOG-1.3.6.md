## 1.3.6

### Fixes
- Fixed a crash when Windows switches between light and dark mode while the app is running. With the app theme on automatic, the app could close and disappear from the tray. The hover card and the update card now follow the new theme without closing the app.
- The tray icon now follows the Windows theme: white on a dark taskbar and black on a light one. It changes as soon as Windows changes, without restarting the app.
